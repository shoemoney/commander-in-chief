## Owner decisions — product calls the run refused to guess at

- **The Riot Shield tells you to FLANK OR GRENADE — and flanking is geometrically impossible in single player, because the shield re-aims at you on every hit test**
  
  WHERE: src/sim/sim_world.gd:3576 `_shield_blocks` — facing is recomputed as `_nearest_alive_player(e.x, e.y) - e.pos` AT THE MOMENT OF THE HIT TEST, with zero turn rate, and the round is deflected when dot(unit_facing, unit_bullet_dir) < -0.5 (a 120-degree front cone). AAA VERSION: give the shield a real heading with a turn rate — it faces its movement direction and rotates toward you at roughly 90 deg/s — so strafing genuinely wins the angle; or latch its facing on a lock that only refreshes every ~1.5 s so there is a readable window on its flank. Either way the promise and the rule agree. If an omni-facing block is genuinely wanted, change the copy to GRENADE IT and stop advertising a flank that cannot happen.
