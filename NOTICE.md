It does **not** grant rights to the bundled art, audio, or other assets. This file is the
scope note; [`ASSETS.md`](ASSETS.md) is the full per-directory provenance map. 🗺️

## 🔊 Audio

The audio under `assets/vo/` and `assets/audio/` is synthesized speech generated for this
project and owned by it.

> ### 🎭 On `assets/vo/` — read this before redistributing
>
> Those lines imitate the voice of an identifiable public figure. The project owns the
> **recordings**; **right of publicity belongs to that person** and is not the project's to
> grant. It was never a licensing question, so no licence — MIT or otherwise — resolves it.
>
> They are published as **satire**, a deliberate decision by the owner. If you redistribute
> or reuse this repository, **that question travels with the audio.** The cleanest way to
> avoid inheriting it is to drop `assets/vo/` from your build; the game runs without it.
